# Similar platforms

Researched November 2026. No one product matches the brief exactly. The
pieces exist, spread across three groups.

## 1. All-in-one employee experience apps (closest match)

| Product | What overlaps with our brief | Gaps vs. our brief |
|---|---|---|
| **Workvivo (by Zoom)** | Social feed, news/articles, chat + push, recognition/shout-outs, badges, surveys, events, employee directory from HRIS | Company news only (no external local/sector news); no games |
| **Blink** | Feed, secure 1:1 and group chat, recognition (Kudos), surveys, SSO, mobile-first for frontline staff | No games; no external news |
| **Haiilo** | Branded intranet app, personalised news feeds, chat, communities, team praise, analytics | Comms-first; light on points/rewards; no games |
| **Refresh** | White-labelled app (own brand), recognition, points, challenges, rewards, social feed | Closest to our resale model; no chat-style DMs or live games |

## 2. Recognition and rewards platforms

| Product | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Vantage Circle** (India) | Recognition, rewards, wellness, surveys; large Indian customer base |
| **Empuls (Xoxoday)** (India) | Social intranet, recognition, rewards catalogue, surveys; priced per active user |
| **Achievers** | Enterprise recognition with a global rewards marketplace, quizzes and polls |
| **Motivosity, WorkTango, Kudos** | Peer recognition where points are redeemable for gift cards |

Published pricing seen in the research: Motivosity $2.25 to $4.50 per user per month. Most others are quote-only.

## What this means for us

- **Our differentiators:** (1) live presence with "invite to play" games inside
  the portal, (2) external news feeds for the office's region and the company's
  industry, (3) a reseller layer, so our client can run it as their own product.
  None of the products above combine all three.
- **Table stakes:** feed, recognition with points, chat, profiles, SSO, a mobile
  app and analytics. Buyers will compare us to Workvivo and Vantage Circle on these.
- **Points need a purpose.** Every recognition competitor turns points into
  rewards (gift cards, vouchers). Without redemption, points lose value quickly.
  Confirm this with the client.
- **Chat risk.** Customers on Teams or Slack may not adopt a second chat app.
  Blink and Workvivo win with frontline staff who have no company chat at all.
  Ask the client which buyers they are targeting.
